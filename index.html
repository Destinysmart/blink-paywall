Put a Lightning paywall on anything

Readers pay in sats or their own currency and the money lands in your Blink wallet. Keep the paid part on your own server, or run it all in the page — the simpler option, but one a determined reader can get around.

Where does the locked content live?

Your paywall server

  1. Deploy it — once, free, about two minutes. The setup asks for your Blink username, a secret key and a password for saving items. Prefer your own machine? It also runs on Node or Deno — instructions.
  2. Connect it here with its address and that password. Your items are then written and priced on this page and saved to your server.

Both stay in this browser only and are sent to nothing but your own server.
